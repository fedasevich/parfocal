## Question

Does the deployed clause of each of the four tasks hold on app.parfocal.eu at release v0.69.0, and what do two 11 tile runs, one outlines only and one typed, take and cost?

## Method

Throwaway Playwright scripts in the agent scratchpad ran against `https://app.parfocal.eu` with the storage state in `~/.config/parfocal/walk069-session.json`, one headless page at a time, as in the walk of v0.66.0 ([2026-11-28-walk-v0.66.0.md](2026-11-28-walk-v0.66.0.md)). They log what they measure and each task is judged from those numbers. No screenshot was opened. The active workspace was `cvut-biomedical` at the start and was left there, so the other two workspaces were not read. The sign in was repeated just before the two runs.

- PROTO-211: the estimates route was read for all 41 slides, then `/projects/{id}/run-model` and the Objects tab of the viewer were opened for CMU-1-Small-Region.svs, test_065 and CMU-1.tiff. This was done before any run.
- PROTO-207 and PROTO-210: test_065 was opened, zoomed by wheel to image point 43476, 24152 at zoom 2^1.29, Smart select armed with the Good model, which runs in the browser. The Typing switch was turned off and on again once. Then: click a cell, two Shift clicks 3 px away, three Cmd+Z, click the cell again, Enter, wait for the typing chip to end, one Cmd+Z, click 90 px to the right, Enter, wait for the chip, key 2. Saved annotations were read from `GET /api/slides/{id}/annotations` after each step.
- PROTO-212: both runs were started from the Run a model window on the one copy of CMU-1-Small-Region.svs (2220 by 2967) in "Scanner format comparison", one after the other. A route guard in the script let a `POST /model-runs` through only with that one slide id, the `pathosam-hovernext` detector and, for the first run, `typer` null. The run was polled every 4 s through `getModelRun`, and the stored cells were read from each result set's class counts and cell summary.

## GPU time and cost

Two model runs (35.3 and 40.8 GPU seconds, $0.0143 together at the estimate's rate) and two typer calls (7.86 and 0.074 GPU seconds by the audit events). The Good outline model ran in the browser and used no server GPU.

`modal billing report --for today` showed the `parfocal-gpu-pool` app at $1.00471486 at 15:14 UTC before the walk, $1.03018031 at about 15:19 UTC just before the runs and $1.15056615 at 15:23 UTC, one minute after the second run finished. The `parfocal-api` app read $0.79148615, $1.18228882 and $1.39503826. So the day's GPU bill grew by $0.1459 during the walk, $0.1204 of it across the two runs. That growth is shared with every other agent that used the pool in those nine minutes and includes cold start and idle time before the scale down, and the report may still lag, so it is an upper bound for this walk and not its cost alone. It is under the 20 cents allowed.
